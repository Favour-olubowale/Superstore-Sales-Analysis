## Dataset
The dataset contains 9994 sales records including revenue, region,product category, etc.

## Data Preparation
To ensure accurate analysis, unnecessary fields such as ZIP code, country, and product name were removed. The dataset represents a single country, and sub-categories provided sufficient product-level detail. Date fields were converted from text to proper date formats, and numerical fields such as sales and profit were converted from text to numbers to enable correct calculations, pivot tables, and charts.

## Observation
- The business generated total sales of 2.29M with a total profit of 286.40K, resulting in a relatively low profit margin of 12%, indicating that profitability is constrained despite strong revenue.
  
- Sales declined in 2020 by approximately 470,533 reflecting a temporary market slowdown. Sales recovered steadily from 2021 and peaked in 2022, making 2022 the strongest sales year within the period analyzed.
  
- A total of 2,204 orders were loss-making, showing that a significant number of transactions negatively impact overall profitability.
  
- Profit contribution varies across categories, with Technology generating the highest profit, while Furniture contributes the least, indicating category Level margin differences.
  
- The West region recorded the highest sales performance, while certain regions with strong sales volumes show comparatively lower profit, highlighting regional pricing or cost inefficiencies.
  
- Shipping times are relatively consistent across regions, but regions with slightly higher average shipping durations tend to align with lower profitability, suggesting an operational cost impact.
  
- The chart shows a strong inverse relationship between discounts and profit margin. As discount levels increase, profit margins consistently decline and eventually become negative, indicating that aggressive discounting directly contributes to losses.


  ## Recommendation
 - Control Discount Levels 
Introduce discount thresholds for low-margin products, as the Discount vs Profit Margin chart shows that profit margins decline sharply and turn negative as discount levels increase.

- Adopt Margin-Based Discounting 
Apply margin based discounting instead of uniform discounts across regions and product categories, since the overall profit margin of 12% indicates limited pricing flexibility, especially in low-margin categories.

- Reevaluate Regional Pricing 
Reassess pricing strategies in regions with high sales but relatively lower profit, as the regional performance chart shows that strong revenue does not always translate into profitability.

- Optimize Shipping Operations 
Improve logistics efficiency in regions with higher average shipping times, as longer shipping durations increase operational costs and contribute to lower profitability.

- Reduce Loss-Making Orders 
Monitor and flag the 2,204 loss-making orders identified in the dashboard to prevent recurring unprofitable transactions and improve overall profit performance.
